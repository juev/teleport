# Teleport privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026.

Teleport is an Android app whose launcher icon opens an app or a link you choose. Questions about this policy can be sent to [denis@evsyukov.org](mailto:denis@evsyukov.org).

## Data on the device

Teleport stores only its configuration: the list of actions (app package names, or links with their labels), their order, and which action the icon tap opens. The configuration lives in the app's private storage.

## Data sent over the network

None. Teleport has no network code and no developer-operated service. Opening a link hands the URL to the app Android picks — the browser, or the app that owns the deep link; that app's own policy applies from there. Launching an app starts it the same way the launcher does.

## Permissions

Teleport requests no Android permissions. It sees the list of launchable apps through the package visibility rules Android provides for launcher-style apps.

## Retention and control

Removing an action deletes it from the configuration. Uninstalling Teleport removes all of its data from the device. Android auto-backup may include the configuration in your own Google backup, under your Android backup settings.

Teleport contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Google Play, F-Droid and Android can process installation, update and device information under their own terms; that is separate from Teleport.

This page will be updated when the app's data handling changes.
